Problem Statement 2: AI Traffic Light Synchronization for Emergency Convoys
Implement an acoustic and visual detection system on traffic lights that detects sirens and emergency beacons 500 meters ahead, creating a dynamic green wave corridor for ambulances.

Problem Statement 3: Underground Storm Drain Overflow & Toxic Gas Monitoring
Create an ATEX-certified, solar-charged sensor array deployed in municipal sewage ducts that measures methane concentrations and water surge levels, alerting storm control centers before street flooding occurs.

Problem Statement 4: Drone Landing Pad Beacon for Medical Supply Drops
Develop an encrypted infrared and radio beacon protocol allowing delivery drones carrying organs or medication to safely identify rooftop drop zones in heavy wind and rain.
